## 8. Custom Side-by-Side Layout (Flexbox)

Use `<div class="flex-container">` and `<div class="flex-item">` for advanced document layouts:

<div class="flex-container">
  <div class="flex-item">
    <h3>Left Column</h3>
    <p>This is standard paragraph text. Notice how it aligns perfectly without breaking the flow.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="flex-item">
    <h3>Right Column</h3>
    <ul class="data-list">
      <li>Perfect for resumes</li>
      <li>Great for comparisons</li>
      <li>Responsive design</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

## 9. Manual Page Breaks

Sometimes you need to force a new page at a specific spot. You can do this by inserting a simple HTML div:

```html
<div class="page-break"></div>
```
